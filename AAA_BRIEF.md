# Fleet Dice 3 AAA quality brief

Build Fleet Dice 3 for phones at the visual and experiential level of the most
polished modern AAA games. The phone is the primary and required play surface;
desktop is not an acceptance target. It should be exceptionally refined,
visually beautiful, and coherent, with every visible and interactive detail held to the same standard:
die faces, materials, lighting, motion, effects, physics, sound, interface,
readability, accessibility, responsiveness, and moment-to-moment game feel.

Fan work out into focused reviews where that improves the result. Give each
major area an owner, iterate on it, and have a separate, demanding visual critic
review screenshots at 390×844 and 402×874. A pass is not complete merely
because it works. It must remain immediately readable during actual play and
look deliberately authored beside the best premium digital tabletop games.

Finish the solo phone experience first. Only after solo passes the mobile
quality gate should its proven layout and interaction changes be ported to
versus mode.

Keep looping through build, real-browser playtest, screenshots, critique, and
revision. Prefer measured improvements over decorative noise. Effects must
support the game state, never obscure numbers or controls. Do not call a visual
pass finished while a die value, resource, action, or outcome is hard to read.
